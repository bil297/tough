FAQ

Questions students ask before ordering

How does the instant quote work?

On the order page you choose the service and your academic level, add your instructions or files, and set your deadline. The site applies our rate card, counts words in uploaded documents where relevant, and shows a fixed price in KES, UGX or USD with the deposit and delivery date. You send it to us on WhatsApp or e-mail and we confirm before any payment.

How do I pay from Kenya, Uganda or South Sudan?

Kenya: M-Pesa. Uganda: MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. South Sudan: USD via MTN MoMo, m-Gurush, bank transfer or PayPal. You pay 50% to start and the balance on delivery; small orders are paid in full. Every payment gets a receipt.

Can you work chapter by chapter?

Yes. Most students order a proposal (chapters 1–3) first, then chapters 4 and 5 after data collection. You pay per chapter as you go and can send supervisor comments between chapters.

Will the work pass plagiarism and AI checks?

Every document is written from scratch and delivered with a similarity and AI-detection report. If you already have a flagged document, we offer a plagiarism and AI check for KES 500 and similarity reduction priced on quote.

Do you do data analysis only?

Yes. Quantitative data (surveys, experiments, secondary datasets) is analysed in SPSS, R or Stata; qualitative data (interviews, focus groups, documents) in NVivo. You get tables, figures, a codebook where relevant and a written interpretation you can use in chapter 4.

What if I have trouble paying?

Talk to us. You can pay chapter by chapter, split a large order into instalments, or have a relative or friend pay on your behalf in Kenya (M-Pesa), Uganda (MoMo) or abroad (PayPal, Wise, Western Union). We will find a way that works for you before any work starts.

What if my supervisor asks for changes?

Revisions within the original instructions are free. Send the comments on WhatsApp and we turn them around quickly, usually within 24 to 48 hours.

Are you recruiting writers?

No. This site is for students and researchers who need support. We are not taking writer applications and messages about writing jobs will not get a reply.
