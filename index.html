FAQ

Questions clients ask before ordering

How do I pay from Kenya or Uganda?

In Kenya, pay via M-Pesa Paybill or Send Money. In Uganda, use MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. Bank transfer and PayPal are also available. You pay 50% to start and the balance on delivery, and every payment gets a receipt.

How fast can you deliver?

Short documents such as CVs and cover letters can be ready in 24 hours. Longer proposals and plans usually take 3 to 7 days. Rush delivery is available at a surcharge shown on the pricing page.

Is my work original and confidential?

Yes. Everything is written from scratch and checked with plagiarism software. We never resell, publish or share your documents, and we sign NDAs on request.

What if I'm not happy with the draft?

You get free revisions for 14 days after delivery. If we miss the agreed deadline, you receive a discount. If we can't deliver what was agreed, you get a refund.

Do you help students write assignments?

We support students with editing, proofreading, formatting, referencing and research coaching. We don't write assessed work for submission as your own, but we will make your own work as strong as it can be.

Can I talk to a real person first?

Of course. Message the Kenya or Uganda WhatsApp line and a coordinator, not a bot, will answer during working hours.
