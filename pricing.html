Pricing FAQ

Common pricing questions

Why is South Sudan quoted in USD?

The South Sudanese pound moves too quickly to publish fixed prices, so we quote in US dollars. You can still pay in SSP via MTN MoMo or m-Gurush at the day's rate, or in USD by bank, PayPal or Western Union.

How long is a chapter?

It depends on your university's guidelines. Typically a proposal chapter is 10 to 25 pages at undergraduate level and longer for master's and PhD. The per-chapter price covers the chapter as your template requires it.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes: order all five chapters together and get 10% off, and returning students get a loyalty discount. Group discounts are available for classmates ordering together.

Is there a charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free and there's no obligation. Use the order page for an instant price or send your instructions on WhatsApp.
