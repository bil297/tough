Pricing FAQ

Common pricing questions

Why is the UGX price rounded?

UGX prices are converted from our KES rate card and rounded to the nearest 500 shillings so they're easy to pay via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Your quote is fixed in UGX once agreed, regardless of exchange-rate changes.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes: 10% off orders above 50 pages, 15% off monthly content packages, and student rates on thesis editing. Returning clients get a loyalty discount on every third project.

Is there a charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free and you're under no obligation. Send the brief on WhatsApp and we'll reply with a price and delivery time.

Can I pay in USD?

Yes, via PayPal or bank transfer. Clients in the diaspora ordering for family or businesses back home do this often.
