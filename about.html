About us

East African researchers helping East African students

Viva Writing Assistance started in Nairobi and now supports students in Kenya, Uganda and South Sudan, at home and studying abroad in the region.

Our story

Good research support shouldn't depend on where you study

We started because too many capable students were stuck: a proposal rejected three times for formatting, data sitting in a questionnaire nobody knew how to analyse, a deadline that collided with a full-time job or a family. The problem was rarely ability. It was time, guidance and access to the right tools.

So we built a team of graduates from East African universities who know the templates, the referencing styles and the defence panels, and made it easy to reach them the way students here actually communicate: on WhatsApp, paying with mobile money.

Today we support students from certificate to PhD in Nairobi, Kampala, Juba and everywhere in between, including South Sudanese students studying in Kenya and Uganda.
