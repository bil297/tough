About us

East African writers, professional standards

Viva Writers started in Nairobi and grew across the border to Kampala. Today we serve clients in every county of Kenya and every region of Uganda.

Our story

Good writing shouldn't depend on where you live

We started because too many strong businesses, capable job-seekers and hard-working students in Kenya and Uganda were losing out on opportunities over documents: a rushed CV, a proposal that didn't follow the donor template, a thesis that read well but was formatted wrong.

So we built a team of writers and editors who know the local context and hold themselves to international standards, and made it easy to reach us in the way East Africans actually communicate: on WhatsApp, paying with mobile money.

Every project is handled by a matched writer and reviewed by an editor before it reaches you. It's a small team on purpose, so you always know who's working on your document.
