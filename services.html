Data analysis: quantitative & qualitative

Send your dataset or transcripts, your questionnaire or interview guide, and your objectives. You get clean output, APA-formatted tables and figures, and a written interpretation that answers each research question and is ready for chapter 4.

Quantitative

  • SPSS: data entry and cleaning, descriptives, reliability (Cronbach's alpha), chi-square, t-tests, ANOVA, correlation, linear and logistic regression
